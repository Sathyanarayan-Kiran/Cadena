The research playbook's status table disagrees with the repository

Cadena Research.docx §7 records a backlog execution status that does not match what the code and tests show. The repository is treated as authoritative throughout this page; the divergences are listed here rather than silently reconciled.

  • Epic 2 is understated. The playbook marks US2.1–US2.3 In Progress. All three are implemented and covered by us2.1–us2.3.
  • Epic 10 is overstated, and it matters. The playbook marks US10.1–US10.2 (SSO and SCIM provisioning) Completed. Neither exists: identity is a header-based stub. The story that is complete is US10.3, role-gated transitions.
  • Epic 4 is fairly described as in progress — US4.1–US4.3 are done, US4.4 has not been started.

Treating the playbook's table as a delivery signal would credit the platform with an enterprise identity posture it does not have.

How status was decided

Done means implemented and covered by an automated acceptance test whose assertions match the story's criteria. Partial means the capability exists but a named part of the acceptance criteria is not met — most often a deliberate pilot boundary, such as notification transports that record rather than send. Not started means no implementation exists.

Eight stories sit at Partial for reasons that are declared boundaries rather than defects: the in-process event bus cannot satisfy the outbox guarantee in US5.1, and the stubbed channel adapters in Epic 8 cannot literally deliver a message. Both are recorded in the specification as pilot stubs, and both keep their stories out of the Done column here.

Eighteen stories were added from Cadena Research.docx: five into existing epics, and four new epics covering bidirectional ServiceNow–Jira synchronization, rich-text fidelity, the agentic AI subsystem, and integration resilience. They are marked New in the ledger.
